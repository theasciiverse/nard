NARD: BACKGAMMON

Each side has 15 men and moves them round the board to its home board, then
bears them off. The first to bear off all 15 wins. You play from the bottom:
your men travel from the top right (24) across the top, down the left and
along the bottom into your home board at the bottom right (points 1-6).

THE THROW

  At the start each side throws one die; the higher plays first, using both
  numbers. After that, each turn begins with a throw of two dice.

  Each die moves one man that many points; one man may use both. Doubles
  are played four times. A man may land on any point not held by two or more
  of the other side's men.

  You must use both dice if you can. If only one can be used, use the higher
  if you can. If neither can be used, the turn passes.

HITTING AND ENTERING

  A lone man on a point (a blot) is hit when a man of the other side lands
  there: it goes to the bar. A man on the bar must enter in the other side's
  home board before any other man may move, on the point its die names
  (a 1 on the 24 point, a 6 on the 19), if that point is open.

BEARING OFF

  Once all 15 of your men are in your home board, a die bears off a man from
  the point it names. A higher die bears off from the highest point when no
  man stands further back. You may also move inside the home board instead.

THE CUBE AND THE SCORE

  A game is worth the value of the cube, which starts at 1 in the middle.
  Before throwing, the side to move may double. The other side either takes,
  and owns the cube at twice the value (only the owner may double next), or
  passes and loses the game at the value before the double.

  A gammon (the loser has borne off no men) counts double; a backgammon (the
  loser has none off and a man on the bar or in the winner's home board)
  counts triple. The score is kept across games for the session.

KEYS

  Arrows or h j k l     move the cursor on the board
  Enter or Space        throw; pick up a man, or put it down
  A S F G Z X ...       pick up a man listed in the panel below the board
  1 - 9                 put the picked man on a listed point
  Esc, U or Backspace   put the man back down, or take back a move
  Enter                 finish the turn, once all dice are used
  D                     double, before throwing
  Y or N                take or pass a double
  O                     the braille ornament on or off
  M                     back to the menu
  F1 or ?               these rules
  Q                     quit

  The mouse works too: click a man to pick it up, a lit point to put it
  down, anywhere to throw, and the dice to finish the turn.

  A man can travel with both dice in one go: pick it up and choose the point
  where it ends; the computer works out the way.

OPTIONS

  --seed N     throw the same dice again
  --demo       the computer plays itself, game after game
  --plain      start without the braille ornament

  RUST_LOG=debug nard 2>game.log keeps a log of the session.

THE OPPONENT

  The computer is bgsage (Open Sage) by Mark Higgins, a neural-network
  engine under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. Hard looks two plies ahead,
  normal one; easy picks among the plays close to the best and never doubles.
